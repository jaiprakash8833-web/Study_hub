🌍 Shaping of the Earth's Surface

📘 About This Chapter

In this chapter, we learn how the Earth's surface changes due to natural forces and processes.

🎯 Learning Goals

  • Understand the interior of the Earth.
  • Learn about tectonic plates.
  • Understand earthquakes and volcanoes.
  • Learn about weathering, erosion and deposition.
  • Identify different landforms.

1. Interior of the Earth

The Earth has three main layers:

Crust: The outermost solid layer of the Earth.

Mantle: The thick layer below the crust.

Core: The innermost part of the Earth.

2. Plate Tectonics

The Earth's outer rigid layer is divided into large pieces called tectonic plates. These plates move slowly.

3. Weathering

Weathering is the breaking down of rocks at or near the Earth's surface.

4. Erosion

Erosion is the wearing away and removal of rock and soil by water, wind, waves or ice.

5. Deposition

Deposition is the process in which transported materials are laid down in a new place.

📝 Quick Revision

The Earth's surface changes due to internal and external forces. Rivers, wind, waves and glaciers help shape different landforms.
